Safety guidance

  • Never pay money to obtain a job unless you independently verify the legitimate service.
  • Do not share passwords, PINs, one-time codes, or sensitive financial information with recruiters.
  • Verify unusual requests directly with the employer using trusted contact details.
  • Report suspicious vacancies, impersonation, scams, or misleading recruitment activity.
